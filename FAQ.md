# Frequently asked questions

Randi here. Short answers to what members ask most. Your organization sets
some of the details, so where it matters I say so.

## What is RandiRandi?

A booking service for shared rooms and things: a guest room, a laundry
slot, a sauna. You see what's free, book it and pay with Vipps. Each
organization has its own bookings, members and rules. It's a service from
Claypond AS.

## How do I sign in?

Enter your email, or your phone number with country code. If you're on your
organization's member list, I email you a code. Type it in and you're in.

## How long does the code last?

10 minutes, and you can use it once. It works only in the browser where you
asked for it, so don't open the email on another device and expect it to
work there. Need a new one? Choose "Request a new code".

## Why was I signed out?

After 30 minutes without activity I sign you out. Ask for a new code and
carry on. "Sign out" in the main navigation does it at once.

## How do I book?

Open Book. Use the Month or the Week view to see what's free. Press
"Filter" to choose what you want to book, which one and how many. Pick a
time and add it to your cart. When the cart is right, go to payment.

## What does it mean to book as a kind of member?

Your organization can offer different things, or different prices, to
different kinds of members. In the Filter you choose which kind you are
booking as. I remember your last choice.

## What is the cart?

Your bookings wait there until you pay. You can remove a time, or add more
before you pay. A small "Go to cart" button follows you around the
calendar once something is in it.

## What is the hold while I pay?

When you go to payment, I hold your times so nobody else can take them.
Right now the hold is 10 minutes. The cart shows how long is left. If it
runs out, the times are released and you can start again. Others see
"Someone is paying" on those times meanwhile.

## How do I pay?

With Vipps. Press "Continue to Vipps", pay, and you come back to your
confirmation. If the payment is still being checked, press "Check again".

## What if the booking is free?

Then there is nothing to pay. Press "Confirm booking" and you're booked.

## How many can I book, and how far ahead?

Each organization sets its own limits, and may set none. There can be a
most-bookings-a-day limit, counted by the day you make the booking, and a
limit on how far ahead you can book, in calendar months.

Example: at the made-up Maple Court Co-op the limits are 3 bookings a day
and 4 months ahead. On 10 March you can book up to 10 July, and make 3
bookings that day. A 4th is refused, and I tell you how many are left.

## How do I cancel?

Open My page, find the booking and press "Ask to cancel". It opens an email
to your organization with the reference filled in. Your organization
cancels it and decides whether to refund. Any refund goes back through
Vipps. If you haven't paid yet, press "Cancel payment" in the cart instead.

## What do I get after booking?

A confirmation email with the times and a calendar file you can open in
your calendar. The confirmation page also has "Add to calendar" and Google
Calendar links.

## Will I get a reminder?

Yes, an email 48 hours before the booking starts. It also lists your other
coming bookings in the five days after. If you book less than 48 hours
ahead, there's no reminder: the confirmation is all you get.

## Who sees my bookings?

You see yours marked "Yours" in the calendar and on My page. Other
members see only that the time is taken, never your name.

## What do you keep about me?

Your name, contact details and bookings, for as long as your organization
has set. Ask your organization if you want to know how long.

## Can I get a copy of my data, or have it erased?

Ask your organization. It handles the request.

## Can I put it on my phone's home screen?

Yes. RandiRandi is a web app, not an app-store app. Open it in your phone's
browser and add it to the home screen from the browser's menu.
